## Security and safety guarantees

- unauthorized sensitive actions do not reach the execution provider;
- account and environment scope isolation is enforced;
- expired role assignments fail closed;
- authorization approval never bypasses SafetyGate;
- KillSwitch blocks risk increase and survives restart;
- KillSwitch is not a hidden flatten mechanism;
- RiskOverride is time-bound;
- RiskOverride cannot bypass KillSwitch;
- RiskOverride cannot override UNKNOWN or INCONSISTENT economic truth;
- stale PostgreSQL governance writers are rejected;
- critical dependency failure closes unsafe admission.

## Operations guarantees

- liveness, operational readiness and trading readiness remain distinct;
- stale health evidence cannot remain green indefinitely;
- critical conditions produce stateful alerts;
- alert sink failure remains observable without erasing alert state;
- telemetry failure cannot alter economic state;
- secret-provider failure fails closed where required;
- no credential material may be emitted through logs, metrics, traces or alerts.

## Deployment baseline

PyTradeKit 0.7.0 qualifies a production-like single-node PAPER deployment profile with:

- externalized secret references;
- PostgreSQL TLS expectations;
- bounded startup;
- bounded shutdown and telemetry flush;
- migration-before-runtime startup;
- container liveness and readiness surfaces;
- non-root container execution;
- read-only filesystem profile;
- explicit Alpaca PAPER endpoint validation.

## Known limitations

- LIVE trading remains unavailable by design.
- The qualified deployment baseline is single-node.
- High-availability orchestration is outside milestone 0.7.
- Cloud-specific secret-manager integrations remain deployment adapters.
- Broker neutrality with a second provider is deferred to milestone 0.8.
- Public APIs remain pre-1.0 and experimental unless explicitly documented otherwise.
