# PyTradeKit 0.7.0a4 - Kill Switch & Risk Overrides

**Status:** Alpha milestone - LOT-04.

PyTradeKit 0.7.0a4 adds governed emergency containment and controlled temporary risk exceptions.

Implemented:

- KillSwitchId, KillSwitch, KillSwitchState, and KillSwitchScope.
- Persistent GLOBAL, ENVIRONMENT, ACCOUNT, and STRATEGY kill switches.
- ActivateKillSwitch and governed ReleaseKillSwitch.
- RiskOverrideId, RiskOverrideScope, RiskOverrideStatus, and explicit expiry.
- RequestRiskOverride, ApproveRiskOverride, CreateRiskOverride, and RevokeRiskOverride.
- DangerousAction and time-bound DangerousActionConfirmation.
- Process-bound confirmation semantics preventing restart resurrection.
- Optimistic concurrency for mutable safety governance aggregates.
- PostgreSQL safety governance schema and repositories.
- Order and strategy safety integration.
- Safety audit evidence through the existing runtime observer.
- FF-040 through FF-042 hard-safety architecture checks.

Qualification proves:

- KillSwitch survives repository restart.
- KillSwitch blocks provider submission even when a matching RiskOverride exists.
- KillSwitch does not flatten positions or submit hidden orders.
- RiskOverride expires deterministically.
- RiskOverride cannot override UNKNOWN or INCONSISTENT economic truth.
- Unauthorized KillSwitch release is denied.
- Governed release, override creation, and override revocation require confirmation and are audited.

LIVE remains unavailable.
