- no Watchdog auto-recovery;
- no time.sleep polling loop;
- no placeOrder or cancelOrder in reconnect runtime;
- no economic replay after reconnect;
- no dynamic LIMIT write before market-rule validation;
- unknown provider states remain fail-closed;
- C3 PAPER certification is not claimed;
- TRADING_READY remains closed;
- LIVE remains unavailable.
