# PyTradeKit 0.8.0b8 - IBKR Real PAPER Certification Harness

**Status:** Beta milestone in progress - LOT-06.

0.8.0b8 adds the real IBKR PAPER evidence pipeline and compliance certification replay.

## Added

- IBKRPaperEvidenceProvenance;
- IBKRPaperEvidenceBundle;
- IBKR runtime-backed certification manifest;
- C2 and C3 compliance requirements and scenarios;
- capability-specific retained evidence validation;
- dynamic C2/C3 certification matrix;
- controlled real PAPER qualification probe;
- SHA-256 evidence normalization;
- certification replay CLI;
- manual self-hosted IBKR PAPER workflow;
- deterministic gate proving CI cannot manufacture C3.

## Safety

- no retained IBKR PAPER evidence is fabricated in CI;
- real-provider proof is mandatory for C3;
- provider IDs are not serialized in retained qualification proof;
- account identity is masked;
- real PAPER orders require explicit acknowledgement;
- workflow is manual only;
- the workflow validates the deterministic CI run belongs to the same commit;
- PARTIAL_FILL remains C2 without a real partial fill;
- LIVE remains unavailable.

## Promotion

A successful real PAPER workflow can provide the evidence required for a future 0.8.0rc1 PAPER-qualified candidate.
