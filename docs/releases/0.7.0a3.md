# PyTradeKit 0.7.0a3 - SafetyState & SafetyDecision

**Status:** Alpha milestone - LOT-03.

PyTradeKit 0.7.0a3 introduces the explicit safety layer above authorization and below business side effects.

Implemented:

- SafetyState: SAFE, DEGRADED, RESTRICTED, HALTED, UNKNOWN.
- SafetyAction and stable SafetyReason codes.
- SafetyEvidence integrating consistency, confidence, recovery, runtime lifecycle, and trading readiness.
- SafetyDecision and deterministic SafetyPolicy.
- Central SafetyGate.
- Orders-owned SafetyGate port for submission and cancellation.
- Risk direction propagated from projected portfolio semantics.
- Strategy admission adapter backed by SafetyGate.
- Fail-closed UNKNOWN and INCONSISTENT handling for risk increase.
- Explicit cancel and reconciliation behavior in restricted states.
- FF-037 provider independence for safety.domain.

Authorization and safety remain separate decisions. Authorization success never bypasses SafetyGate.

LIVE remains unavailable.
